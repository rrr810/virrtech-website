FAQ

Asked before starting

Who owns the website when it's done?
You do — code, domain, accounts. Everything is opened in your name from day one and credentials are shared at kickoff. That is policy, not a favour.
What if I change my mind mid-project?
You pay for completed milestones; the unused portion is refunded within 14 days. Before kickoff, a full refund minus bank fees. If we fail to deliver, we redo the work free or refund — your choice.
What if I ask for extras later?
The written quote defines scope. Anything beyond it becomes a change order: quoted within 2 business days, approved in writing (WhatsApp counts), then built. Tweaks under 30 minutes during the build are free.
Why do custom systems cost more than websites?
A website says who you are. A system runs your operations — stock, members, payments — for years. Different depth, different value. Custom work is scoped first (KES 30,000, fully credited) so you never pay for guesswork.
My budget is very small. Any options?
Launch Kits at KES 6,000–10,000 (48 hours to 5 days), or a Google Business Profile setup at KES 5,000. Both get you findable this week; kit fees are credited in full if you later upgrade.
