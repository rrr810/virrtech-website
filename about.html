Why VirrTech exists

Good ideas kept dying at the same question: who will build it? The salon owner with a phone full of booking messages. The trader with stock in a notebook. The founder with a deck and no product.

VirrTech Solutions is the answer to that question — the technology division of VirrTech Group, founded in Nairobi to build practical digital systems for Kenyan businesses, organizations and entrepreneurs, and to stay involved after launch.
