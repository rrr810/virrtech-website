SokoShamba

Agricultural marketplace · farmers → buyers → transporters

Context. Kenyan agricultural trade runs on fragmented channels: price discovery is weak, supply is inconsistent, transport is a gamble. We set out to fix the loop, not a single side of it.

Decisions. A three-sided marketplace: farmers sell direct, buyers source with confidence, transporters get predictable work. Trade made more accessible, transparent and efficient — end to end.

Build. Full product by our team: roles and accounts, listings and discovery, order flow, payment and verification plumbing. Deployed on a modern stack.

Outcome. A live platform we operate ourselves — and the reason "can you build a marketplace?" gets a short answer.
